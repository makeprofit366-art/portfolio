5 pins delivered in 2 days. Weekly packages delivered daily. Monthly packages get 5 pins every single day — always fresh content ready to post.

What do I need to provide?

Just your niche, brand colors, website link and topic to promote. If you have logos or fonts, send those too. I handle everything else!

Do you provide revisions?

Yes! Every order includes unlimited revisions. I want you to absolutely love the final design. Colors, text, layout — just say the word!

Do you offer a free sample?

Yes! Click "✨ Free Sample" at the top, tell me your niche, and I'll design a sample pin for you. See the quality before you commit — no strings attached!

What file formats will I receive?

You'll receive high-resolution PNG files ready to upload directly to Pinterest, sized at the optimal 1000×1500px (2:3 ratio). Starter packs also include editable PSD source files.

The pins I design for you are yours to use on your own platforms. For agency or resale rights, just message me and we'll work out the right arrangement!
