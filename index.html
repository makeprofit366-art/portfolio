Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Starter (15 pins): 1 business day. Growth (50 pins): 2 business days. Monthly plan: 5 fresh pins delivered every day. Rush same-day orders are also available — just ask.

What do I need to provide?

Just your store or blog link, your niche, and any brand colors or fonts you use. That's it. I handle the keyword research, pin titles, and all the design from there.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes — all plans include unlimited revisions. I work until you are 100% happy with every design. Colors, layout, text, style — just tell me what needs changing and I'll fix it.

What file format do I receive?

High-resolution PNG files at 1000×1500px (the optimal 2:3 Pinterest ratio), ready to upload immediately. Growth plan and above also includes editable PSD source files.

Is the free sample really free?

Completely free. No payment, no credit card, no commitment whatsoever. You send your link, I design 2 pins, you post them. If you want more we continue — if not, no hard feelings at all.

Do you do keyword research for every pin?

Yes, every single pin. Each design comes with a suggested title and target keyword so you know exactly what to write in the pin description to maximize your search visibility.

I'm new to Pinterest. Will this still work?

Absolutely — this is actually the best time to start. A brand-new Pinterest account with well-designed, SEO-optimized pins will grow faster than an existing account with poor designs.

Can I order for multiple niches?

Yes. If you run multiple stores or boards, I can split your pin order across niches. Just mention this when you message me and we'll plan it out together — no extra charge.
