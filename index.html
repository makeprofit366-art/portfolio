Starter (15 pins): 1 business day. Growth (50 pins): 2 business days. Monthly: 5 fresh pins delivered daily. Rush orders can be done same-day.

What do I need to provide?

Just your store/blog link, your niche, and any brand colors or fonts you use. That's all. I handle the keyword research, titles, and design entirely.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes — all plans include unlimited revisions. I work until you're 100% happy with the designs. Colors, layout, text — just say what needs changing.

What file format do I receive?

High-resolution PNG files at 1000×1500px (2:3 ratio) — the optimal Pinterest format, ready to upload. Growth plan and above also includes editable PSD files.

Is the free sample really free?

Yes, completely free. No payment, no credit card, no commitment. You send your link, I design 5 pins, you post them. If you want more, we continue — if not, no hard feelings.

Do you do keyword research for every pin?

Yes. Every pin design comes with a suggested title and target keyword — so you know exactly what to write in the pin description to maximize search visibility.

I'm new to Pinterest. Will this still work?

Absolutely — actually the best time to start. A brand-new Pinterest account with well-designed, SEO-optimized pins will grow faster than an existing one with poor designs.

Can I order for multiple niches?

Yes. If you run multiple stores or boards, I can split your pin order across niches. Just mention this when you message me and we'll plan it out together.
