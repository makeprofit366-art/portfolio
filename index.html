How long does delivery take?

Starter (15 pins): 1 business day. Growth (50 pins): 2 business days. Monthly: 5 fresh pins delivered every day. Rush orders can be completed same-day — just mention it when you message me.

What do I need to provide?

Just your store or blog link, your niche, and any brand colours or fonts you use. That's all. I handle the keyword research, title writing, and the full design from start to finish.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes — all plans include unlimited revisions. I work until you're genuinely 100% happy with every design. Colours, layout, text, fonts — just tell me what needs changing and I'll fix it.

What file format do I receive?

High-resolution PNG files at 1000×1500px (2:3 ratio) — the optimal Pinterest format, ready to upload immediately. Growth plan and above also includes editable PSD source files.

Is the free sample really free?

Yes — completely free. No payment, no credit card, no commitment at all. You send your link, I design 5 pins for you, and you post them. If you want more, we continue. If not, no hard feelings whatsoever.

Do you do keyword research for every pin?

Yes, always. Every pin design comes with a suggested title and target keyword — so you know exactly what to write in the pin description when you upload it to maximise search visibility.

I'm new to Pinterest. Will this still work?

Absolutely — in fact, starting fresh is often an advantage. A brand-new Pinterest account with well-designed, SEO-optimised pins will grow faster than an existing account built on poor designs. The best time to start is now.

Can I order for multiple niches?

Yes. If you run multiple stores, boards, or niches, I can split your pin order across them. Just mention it when you first get in touch and we'll plan the split together.
