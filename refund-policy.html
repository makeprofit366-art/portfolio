Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 2025  ·  Effective immediately

✓  We stand behind every pin we design. If you're not satisfied, we offer unlimited revisions and a fair refund process. Our goal is always your 100% satisfaction.

1. Refund Eligibility by Timeline

✓
Within 48 hours of payment — Full refund available
If work has not yet started, you are entitled to a full refund, no questions asked. Email us at support@clickablepins.online with your order details.
~
After work begins — Revision first, then partial refund
If work has started and you're unhappy with the designs, we will first offer unlimited revisions to make them right. If after revisions you remain unsatisfied, a partial refund may be issued based on the work completed.
✗
After delivery and approval — No refund
Once pins have been delivered and approved (or revision requests completed), no refund is available. Digital design files are non-returnable after delivery and use.

2. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are not available in the following situations:

  • Free sample pins (the 2 free pins offer has no monetary value)
  • Change of mind after design has been delivered and approved
  • Pinterest account performance issues (we design pins but cannot control the Pinterest algorithm)
  • Monthly plan — refunds are only available for unused future months, not the current billing period
  • Orders cancelled after revision requests have been satisfied

3. Cancellations

3.1 One-Time Orders (Starter & Growth Plans)

You may cancel your order at any time before work begins for a full refund. Once design work has started, cancellations are subject to a partial refund based on the percentage of work completed.

3.2 Monthly Plan

You may cancel your monthly subscription at any time by emailing support@clickablepins.online. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. We do not offer pro-rata refunds for unused days within a billing period.

3.3 Cancellation by PinStudio

We reserve the right to cancel an order if the requested content violates our Terms & Conditions. In this case, a full refund will be issued.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or cancellation:

  • Email us at support@clickablepins.online
  • Include your name, order date, and the email address used to pay
  • Describe the reason for your refund request

We will respond within 1–2 business days and process approved refunds within 5–10 business days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method (Razorpay or Stripe). Bank processing times may add 3–7 additional business days depending on your bank or card issuer.

5. International Payments

For international customers paying via Stripe (USD/EUR/GBP etc.):

  • Refunds are processed in the same currency as the original payment
  • Currency fluctuations between payment and refund date are not our responsibility
  • Stripe processing fees (typically 2.9% + $0.30) may not be refunded for partial refunds
  • Refund timeline via Stripe: typically 5–10 business days to your card or account

For Indian customers paying via Razorpay (INR):

  • Refunds via Razorpay typically process within 5–7 business days
  • UPI refunds: 1–3 business days
  • Net Banking refunds: 3–5 business days
  • Card refunds: 5–10 business days

6. Disputes

If you are unhappy with our response to a refund request, please email support@clickablepins.online with "DISPUTE" in the subject line. We will escalate the matter to senior review within 48 hours. We always prefer to resolve disputes directly rather than through payment processor chargebacks.

⚠️  Please contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank or card issuer. Chargebacks create unnecessary complications for both parties. We are always willing to find a fair resolution directly.
