Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to deliver a pin?

Single pins are delivered within 24–48 hours. Pin packs (5 pins) are typically ready in 3–4 days. Monthly packages are delivered in weekly batches so you always have fresh content ready to post.

What information do I need to provide?

Just share your niche, brand colors, blog/website link, and the topic or product you want to promote. If you have specific fonts or logos, send those too. I'll take care of the rest and create pins that perfectly match your style.

Do you provide revisions?

Yes! Every order includes up to 2 free revisions. I want you to absolutely love the final design. If you need changes to colors, text, layout, or anything else — just let me know and I'll fix it quickly.

What file formats will I receive?

You'll receive high-resolution PNG files optimized for Pinterest (1000×1500px — the ideal 2:3 ratio). I can also provide JPG or PDF formats on request. All files are ready to upload directly to Pinterest without any extra editing.
