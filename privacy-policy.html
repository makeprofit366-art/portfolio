Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025  ·  Effective immediately

At PinStudio (clickablepins.online), we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We will never sell your personal information.

1. Who We Are

PinStudio is a Pinterest pin design service operated at clickablepins.online. We are the data controller for personal information you provide us. For privacy-related questions, contact us at: support@clickablepins.online

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Name and email address (from our contact form)
  • Your business type and store/blog URL
  • Message content describing your products or services
  • Brand assets (logos, images, color codes) shared for design purposes
  • Payment information (processed securely by Razorpay/Stripe — we never store card details)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address and browser type (via server logs)
  • Pages visited and time spent (via analytics)
  • Referring website or search terms used to find us
  • Device type (desktop/mobile/tablet)

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your data. We share it only with trusted third-party service providers:

  • Razorpay — payment processing for Indian customers (INR)
  • Stripe — payment processing for international customers
  • Web3Forms — contact form submission handling
  • Google Analytics — anonymous website usage statistics
  • Cloud hosting providers — to serve our website

All third-party providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only for the services they provide to us.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Client project files and communications: 2 years after last order
  • Payment records: 7 years (required by law for financial records)
  • Marketing emails: until you unsubscribe
  • Website analytics: 26 months (Google Analytics default)

After the retention period, your data is securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — request we correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request we delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to certain uses of your data
  • Withdraw consent — unsubscribe from marketing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@clickablepins.online. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function
  • Analytics cookies — understand how visitors use our site (Google Analytics)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking analytics cookies won't affect your ability to use our services.

8. International Transfers

If you are located outside India, your information may be transferred to and processed in India or other countries where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data including HTTPS encryption, secure payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant via Razorpay/Stripe), and limited staff access to personal data. No method of transmission is 100% secure — if you believe your data has been compromised, contact us immediately.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify active clients of significant changes by email. The updated policy will always be posted on this page with a revised date.
